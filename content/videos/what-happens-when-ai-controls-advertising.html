This scenario looks ordinary because it is ordinary: a marketing AI writes an email. Without relational ethics, the model leans into urgency, scarcity, and pressure. The customer becomes a target to be manipulated.

With the relational ethics frame, the model notices the customer as a person, not just a conversion. It shifts toward honesty, transparency, and respect.

AI can optimize persuasion until people become pressure points, but relational ethics can make human dignity part of the system's reasoning.
