## Education and Direct Programs

AEI develops education and direct programs on responsible AI use, with emphasis on vulnerable and underserved populations. This includes people navigating intense relationships with AI systems, approached with care and clinical seriousness rather than dismissal or hype.

Program details are forthcoming. For inquiries about program partnerships, [contact us](mailto:contact@alignmentethics.org).
